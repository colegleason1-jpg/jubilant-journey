# Inbound watch intake

This is the chargeback insurance policy. Every step produces evidence.

**Scale the ceremony to the value.** Below $500 the handling budget is minutes, not
half an hour — 5 photos, no video. The full SOP below is for $500+.

## Do this

1. **Start recording before cutting the tape.** One continuous take through step 5.
2. **Serial number** — photograph it, read it aloud on camera, log it to
   `inventory.serial_number`. *Highest-value 60 seconds in the operation.* It's what
   defeats the buy-genuine/return-fake swap.
3. **Inspect against the source listing** — dial, hands, indices, case, caseback,
   crystal, bracelet, clasp, crown action, timekeeping (±sec/day), box/papers.
4. **Does it match?** If no → stop, return to the seller (eBay Money Back Guarantee
   covers counterfeit and not-as-described for 30 days and overrides the seller's own
   policy). Refund the customer if an order is attached.
5. **Photograph: 20 shots**, consistent lighting — front, back, both sides, clasp open
   and closed, bracelet, lume, on-wrist, macro dial, macro caseback, box and papers,
   and **every flaw individually and close**.
6. **Stop recording.** Upload video and photos to the order's evidence pack.

## Rules

- **Document flaws aggressively.** The scratch you disclosed is a selling point; the
  scratch you hid is a chargeback you lose.
- **Under-promise condition.** A customer delighted it's better than described never
  files a dispute.
- Colour and exposure correction is fine. Editing out marks is not — see
  `photos.ts`, which blocks it.
- Photos must be of **this** unit unless the watch is genuinely new/unworn and the
  imagery is licensed. Same rule we reject eBay sellers on.

## Output

Confirm what was recorded: serial, photo count, condition notes, flaws found, whether
it matches the listing, and whether it's cleared to list. Flag anything that should
go back.
