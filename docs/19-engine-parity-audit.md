# 19 — Engine / scanner parity audit

`packages/core/src/*.ts` is where the logic is designed, documented and unit-tested.
`services/scout/scout/*.py` is what actually runs on cron against live eBay data.
CLAUDE.md requires them to agree. They do not.

This matters before shadow mode rather than after, because shadow mode costs three
weeks of waiting, and calibrating a scanner that decides differently from the
documented engine spends those weeks fitting thresholds to the wrong system.

Six dimensions audited; every finding independently verified against the files by a
second agent. **61 confirmed, 4 rejected on verification.** Four are fixed;
57 remain open below.

## Fixed

| Finding | What it was |
|---|---|
| `AUTHENTICATION_ELIGIBLE` rejected everything under $500 | The **second** instance of the $500 floor in CLAUDE.md's mistakes table. 19 of 29 references are under $500 retail. The test guarding that floor only checked `PRICE_BAND`, so this gate re-imposed it invisibly for months. |
| `score_gates` used `sold_price × 0.90 − landed` | Omitted shipping, rail fee and packaging, so the grader was kinder than the engine it grades. On the demo it called five deals that each **lost $14.03** "profitable rejects worth $225", concluding *loosen the gates*. |
| `shadow_decisions` counted per row | The scanner re-records its best candidate every run, so an unsold listing contributed hundreds of rows and a fast sale one — weighting calibration by how long a deal failed to sell. |
| `calibrate.py` read the newest 5,000 rows | A three-week report silently covered only its tail. |
